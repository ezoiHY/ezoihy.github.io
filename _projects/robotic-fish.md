## Underwater perception and collaboration

I am participating in a national-level student innovation project on underwater human–robot collaboration. The project uses a vision-guided biomimetic robotic fish to investigate scene recognition and diver tracking.

## My contributions

- Responsible for visual recognition and diver tracking.
- Contributed to the algorithm architecture.
- Developed a scene-recognition module based on **YOLOv8**.
- Assisted with improvements to the fish's mechanical structure.

This work has given me experience with visual algorithms within a complete robot system and helped me understand the connections between perception, algorithm architecture, and mechanical design.
