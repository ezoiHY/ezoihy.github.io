Harry Han

Robotics Engineering · South China University of Technology

I am an undergraduate studying Robotics Engineering at South China University of Technology in Guangzhou, China.

My interests lie in dexterous manipulation, underactuated mechanisms, vision-based robot control, and embodied intelligence. I am interested in how simpler mechanisms and control methods can make robots more reliable and affordable.

My projects include an underactuated prosthetic hand, monocular hand-motion retargeting, a biomimetic robotic fish, and language-driven robot interaction. These projects span mechanical design, perception, simulation, and control.

I started with six years of programming and informatics competitions before moving into mechanical design and robotics. This site brings together my research, engineering projects, and occasional notes on what I am learning.
