# AI project cover prompts

Created October 15, 2026 with the built-in `image_gen` editing tool. Each call used only the corresponding owner-supplied photograph as its edit target. These prompts describe the requested edit; they do not establish that a generated cover is an unmodified technical photograph. The original photographs remain the detail-page images.

The two generated PNGs were each 1672 × 941. They were encoded as WebP at quality 90, method 6, without further resizing or cropping. Thumbnail containers use 16:9 and `object-fit: contain`.

## Underactuated prosthetic hand

Input: `underactuated-hand-photo.webp`.

Output: `underactuated-hand-cover.webp`.

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit target: the supplied photograph of a real white tendon-driven prosthetic hand gripping a clear plastic bottle on a black optical bench. Deliver ONE photographic website project-cover image, exact 16:9 landscape framing, ideally 2048 x 1152 pixels. This is a BACKGROUND OUTPAINT, not a redesigned robot or a new photograph. Preserve the ENTIRE supplied photograph in the central area without cropping or distorting it; extend the existing matte black backdrop and optical bench naturally on the left and right to make it wide. Preserve the prosthetic hand, all five fingers and their springs, screws, tendons, wrist mount and complete base, the bottle with its exact shape, reflections, and red cap, the original pose, texture, lighting, and camera angle exactly. No extra components, altered geometry, extra fingers, duplicate objects, text, graphics, watermarks, borders or letterbox bars. The extended empty background must blend seamlessly with the original black scene; the original hand, bottle and base must remain fully visible and sharp. Keep the scene realistic and restrained for an academic engineering portfolio.
```
